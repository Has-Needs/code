# Need Configurator — planned prototype

**Status:** Design fixture only; **no executable prototype or experimental results yet**.

**Prototype aim:** Implement the provided, literacy-agnostic graphical controls for expressing a Has or Need and selecting from the bounded official vocabulary of basic giving, receiving, and exchange contract primitives. Participants supply the meaning and contents; they do not author the interface or design a contract to perform basic actions.

The Need itself is the smart contract—not a separate agreement object or execution room. Locally meaningful ontology content, relevant conditions, and authorized disclosure are captured through the supplied controls. Advanced participants may write custom contract extensions, without those becoming official primitives. Accepted exchanges enter `WORKING` and later follow the independent ready-to-commit flow.
